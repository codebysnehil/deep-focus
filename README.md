# Deep Focus ⏱️

A distraction-free, fullscreen **flip-clock countdown timer** for deep work. Pick 1 h, 2 h, 5 h (or any custom length), hit start, have a productive session.

**Live demo:** : https://deep-focus-work.vercel.app/

<img width="1915" height="936" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cb4718cd-ccb9-4486-97e2-61426e27a731" />

## Features)
- 1 / 2 / 5 hour presets + custom minutes (up to 999)
- Live current time and date
- Focus mode: controls and cursor fade out after 4 s of inactivity while running
- Light / dark theme (follows system, remembers your choice)
- Keyboard-first: `Space` start/pause · `R` reset · `F` fullscreen · `D` theme
